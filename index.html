How we clean fabric

A four-step method that lifts dirt out without soaking the foam underneath.

  1. Dry vacuumRemoves hair, dust and loose dirt before any moisture is used.
  2. Pre-spray and agitateEco-friendly cleaner and soft brushes break down body oils and food stains.
  3. 100°C steamHot steam sanitizes deep into the fibres, killing dust mites, bedbugs and bacteria.
  4. Wet extractionA strong vacuum pulls out the dirty water. Fabric dries in about 2 hours.

Keep your car looking new

A few habits that make every wash and detail last longer.

Paint

Wash regularly so dust and bird droppings do not bake into the paint. Buffing every so often restores shine that daily dust wears down.

Headlights

Sun and dust cloud headlights over time. Restoring them early costs less than replacing them, and it improves night visibility.

Interior

Vacuum seats and mats often and wipe spills straight away. Stains are far easier to remove while fresh.
